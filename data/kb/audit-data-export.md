# Full data export for audits

Customers can request a complete export of their work orders, technician assignments and timestamps for audits.

1. Use Reports > Scheduled exports > "Full history". Choose the date range (e.g. the 2025 calendar year).
2. The export includes technician ids, not names, unless "include names" is ticked by an admin.
3. Exports are kept for 7 days and the download link requires sign-in.

Support should not send export files by email.
