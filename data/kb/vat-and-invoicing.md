# VAT, sales tax and invoicing

- EU customers are charged VAT at their country's rate unless they provide a valid VAT ID (reverse charge).
- UK customers are charged 20% VAT unless they provide a VAT number.
- US and Canadian entities are not charged VAT. US sales tax is applied only in states where Kestrel Cloud has nexus (currently none).
- A US entity invoiced with VAT gets a corrected invoice; see "Refund and billing corrections policy".

Invoices are issued on the first day of each billing period in USD or EUR.
