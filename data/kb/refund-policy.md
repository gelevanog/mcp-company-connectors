# Refund and billing corrections policy

## Duplicate charges

If a customer was charged twice for the same period, the duplicate payment is refunded in full to the original payment method within 5 business days. Support creates an internal note with the payment ids and assigns the ticket to the billing queue (an admin). Never ask the customer for full card numbers.

## Plan downgrades

Downgrades take effect at the next renewal; we do not refund the remainder of an annual term.

## Corrected invoices

Invoices with a wrong tax rate, address or entity are re-issued within 3 business days. The corrected invoice replaces the original; the customer does not need to pay twice.

## Who approves

Refunds above 10,000 USD need approval by the finance lead. Support never promises a refund amount before billing confirms it.
