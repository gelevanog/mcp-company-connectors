# Escalation process

1. The assignee confirms impact (users affected, workaround available).
2. For urgent tickets, page the on-call engineer and open an incident (INC-...) within 30 minutes.
3. Post an update on the status page for incidents that affect more than one customer.
4. The account owner (sales) is informed of every urgent ticket on an Enterprise account.
5. After resolution, write a short post-incident note and link it on the ticket.

Only the support lead or an admin may close an urgent ticket.
