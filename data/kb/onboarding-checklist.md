# Customer onboarding checklist

1. Kick-off call with the customer's operations lead; agree on the go-live date.
2. Import technicians, skills and service areas.
3. Configure SSO (Growth and Enterprise).
4. Pilot with one depot for two weeks.
5. Train dispatchers (2 hours) and technicians (30 minutes in the app).
6. Go live depot by depot; weekly check-ins for the first month.
