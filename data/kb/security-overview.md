# Security overview

- SOC 2 Type II report available under NDA.
- Data encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256).
- Data residency: US (Virginia) or EU (Frankfurt).
- Customer data is never used to train AI models.
- Bulk exports of customer or contact lists are only possible for admins and are logged.
