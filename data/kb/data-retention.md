# Data retention

- Completed jobs and their photos: kept for the life of the contract, then deleted 90 days after termination.
- Customers who need longer retention (e.g. seven years) can enable Extended retention (Enterprise).
- Audit logs: 2 years.
- Backups: 35 days.
