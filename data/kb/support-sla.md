# Support SLA and response times

Kestrel Cloud answers every ticket within the first-response target for its priority. Targets apply 24/7 for Enterprise and during business hours (08:00-18:00 in the customer's region) for Growth and Starter.

| Priority | First response | Update cadence | Typical examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| Urgent | 1 hour | every 2 hours | dispatch or mobile app down for many users, data loss |
| High | 4 hours | daily | a core feature broken for a team, crashes affecting many technicians |
| Normal | 1 business day | every 3 business days | a feature not working as expected, slow exports |
| Low | 2 business days | weekly | questions, how-tos, billing address changes |

## Breaches

A ticket that misses its first-response target is flagged in the support backlog report. The support lead (Tara Lindqvist) reviews breached tickets every morning.

## Escalation

Enterprise customers can escalate any ticket to urgent by phone. See the article "Escalation process".
