# Using AI assistants with Kestrel data

Kestrel staff may connect AI assistants to the CRM, helpdesk and analytics through Switchboard only.

- The assistant sees only the tools your role allows.
- Every change (a note, a ticket update, an email) is shown to you for confirmation first.
- Emails are never sent directly: they go to an outbox that an admin approves.
- Text written by customers (tickets, pasted emails, community articles) may contain instructions aimed at the assistant. They are marked as untrusted; report anything suspicious to security@kestrel.example.
