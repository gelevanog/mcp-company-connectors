# Salesforce integration

Syncs accounts and contacts from Salesforce every 15 minutes and work orders back as a custom object.

## Accounts stopped syncing

Most often the integration user's password expired or its permission set was removed. Re-authorize under Settings > Integrations > Salesforce.
